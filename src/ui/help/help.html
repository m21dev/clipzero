Quick start (3 steps)

ClipZero runs automatically on 55 AI chat, messaging, social, developer, and webmail sites on both Firefox and Chromium.

1

Open a supported site

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp Web, Discord, GitHub, and 47 more sites are protected out of the box.

2

Paste or upload as usual

ClipZero intercepts paste, drag-and-drop, and file uploads in RAM before the page reads them—your original files on disk are never changed.

3

Review, restore, or customize

Use the toolbar popup to toggle options per tab, inspect active session tokens, or open Rules & Backup for custom patterns.

Secrets, custom words & custom rules

Choose the right tool depending on what you want to hide when pasting or uploading text/code files:

Automatic Secret & PII Guard

Enabled by default on AI chats. Detects API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, AWS, GitHub), JWTs, payment cards, IBANs, URL passwords, and optional emails, phones, or IPs.

Custom hidden words (per site)

In the popup Settings tab, list exact names, companies, or projects separated by commas (up to 50 per site). Best for fixed names without patterns.

Custom rules (all 55 sites)

In Rules & Backup, pick a 1-click template or use the Guided builder (no regex knowledge needed) to hide structured codes like EMP-104298 or INC-84920 everywhere.

Tip: Masked placeholders like [REDACTED_OPENAI_KEY] stay reversible in tab RAM only. Open the popup Tokens tab to inspect or clear them, or copy a tokenized reply to get a 1-click “Restore originals” button.

Visual Canvas Redactor (screenshots)

Enable “Redact before upload” in the popup (or press Alt+Shift+B) to inspect and censor images inside a private Shadow DOM modal before uploading.

O Outline — highlight box (Thin / Med / Thick)

Editor keys: Arrows / Shift+Arrows move or resize the selected box · Del removes it · Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes · PageUp/PageDown switches batch images · Enter uploads.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the ClipZero toolbar icon show an orange “!” badge?

That tab was already open before you installed or updated ClipZero. Because ClipZero hooks paste and upload APIs at document_start for security, simply reload that tab once (or click “Reload tab” in the popup) to activate protection.

Why does Firefox ask me to grant access to supported sites?

In Firefox Manifest V3, host permissions can start disabled depending on browser settings. Click the “Allow” banner in the popup or Welcome page once to grant access to the 55 supported sites—ClipZero still requests zero API permissions.

How do I hide custom codes if I don’t know regular expressions?

Open Rules & Backup and click any 1-click template (Employee ID, Ticket, Client code, Invoice, Internal token) or use the Guided builder: enter the prefix, character type, and length, test it in the live sandbox, and save.

Where are my tokens and rules stored, and does anything leave my device?

Masked session tokens exist only in volatile tab RAM and vanish when you reload, close the tab, or click Clear vault. Your saved per-site preferences and custom rules stay in extension-isolated IndexedDB. ClipZero makes zero network calls and collects zero telemetry.

Why wasn’t a legacy .doc or password-protected Office file modified?

ClipZero cleans modern OOXML archives (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx) losslessly. Legacy binary OLE2 files (.doc/.xls/.ppt) and encrypted documents are intentionally passed through untouched so they are never corrupted (filename anonymization still applies when enabled).
